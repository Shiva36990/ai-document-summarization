Title: The Impact of Excessive Mobile Phone Usage

Excessive mobile phone usage, often termed "smartphone addiction," has become a significant modern issue. While smartphones are essential tools, overusing them leads to several negative consequences:

1. Physical Health: Constant screen time causes eye strain, headaches, and poor posture ("text neck"). Poor sleep quality is common due to blue light emission before bed.
2. Mental Health: Overuse can lead to increased anxiety, depression, and FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out).
3. Productivity & Focus: Constant notifications break concentration, leading to poor academic performance for students and reduced efficiency at work.
4. Social Life: Real-life relationships suffer as people prioritize digital interaction over face-to-face communication.

How to Reduce Usage:
- Set daily screen time limits using built-in tools.
- Create "phone-free" zones (e.g., dining table, bedroom).
- Turn off non-essential notifications.
- Re-engage with offline hobbies.

Conclusion:
Balancing technology use is key to maintaining a healthy lifestyle.
